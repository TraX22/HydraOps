- 🔑 **An X account is connected for a purpose.** Connecting an account now starts with a picker: read (always), publish, engage (like, repost, bookmark, follow, mute, block) and messages. HydraOps asks X for the permissions of those uses only, so an account connected to publish can never be used for anything else, whatever a model says. Each account's row shows its uses; one is switched off there at any time, and adding one connects the account again with it ticked, because X grants permissions only at sign-in. Accounts connected before keep reading and publishing.
- 👀 **The agents read X.** `x_read` brings an account's mentions (only the new ones with `since_id`), its own posts and their author-only metrics, its timeline, a profile, a user's posts, one post, its thread, who quoted, liked or reposted it, its likes and bookmarks; `x_search` searches the last seven days or the archive with X's query syntax and counts matches per day, hour or minute. Reads are not held for approval, but what they return is outside content, like a web page: a mention that tries to give the agent orders is reported to you, not obeyed, and a sensitive action after it waits for you. Every operation comes from one closed table in the key-proxy; nothing outside it reaches X.
- 💸 **Money keeps reads in check.** Every read costs the developer account, so every tool answer ends with what that call cost by X's price list and where the money stands. Two limits, both in the card: a read budget per task (0.50 USD unless you change it; past it, reads are refused and the agent is told) and a monthly cap per account (empty by default; the row shows the month's spend with a bar, and posts count too). The estimate is HydraOps'; X's portal has the real figure.
- ❤️ **The agents act on X.** `x_engage` likes, reposts, bookmarks (and undoes them), deletes an own post and hides a reply; `x_users` follows, blocks, mutes (and undoes them) and reads followers, following, blocked and muted. Every action waits for your approval like a post does; unlike a post, "approve for the rest of the task" covers the next calls of the same tool, so "like these five posts" asks once. The card and the Telegram notice say what the agent wants to do, to which post or user, as which account, and X's price. The server runs only the exact call you approved: an approved like cannot become anything else.
- 💬 **A post can reply, quote, poll and choose who replies.** `x_post` takes `reply_to` (the way to answer a mention), `quote`, a poll of two to four options and who may reply. They go with the first part, the card shows them, and they are part of what you approved, live or scheduled through the Calendar.
- 🔔 **An account can be listened to.** Switch "Listen for mentions" on in an account's row, choose which of its agents handles them and how often to check (15 minutes by default, 5 at the least). Every new mention becomes a task for that agent, the way a Telegram message does, with the text as outside content and the mention as the task's origin; Telegram tells you who wrote and what; the agent decides whether to reply, react or leave it, and whatever it decides waits for you. Switching it on starts from the newest mention, nothing older is replayed; a check with nothing new costs nothing. The row shows the last check, the counts, any error, and a "check now" button.
