- 🎬 **Videos on X.** `x_post` now takes a video (an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, such as the ones ComfyUI makes) as a post's attachment, or a GIF of up to 15 MB. The video is uploaded in pieces and X processes it before the post goes out, so a post with a video takes minutes; the approval card plays it. A video or a GIF goes alone in its part; images stay at four per part.
- ✔️ **X Premium accounts.** Tick "X Premium account" on an account in Tools → X and the agents may publish long posts (up to 25,000 characters instead of 280) and long videos on it; the approval card and the Telegram notice count against that limit.
- 🧠 **Conversation memory per agent.** Each agent has a setting for how much of the earlier chat goes with every new message: none, short, normal or long (the default, as before). Earlier chat is sent again on every step of a task, so an agent whose requests stand on their own works noticeably cheaper with less.
