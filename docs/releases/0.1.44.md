- 🔒 **Security update.** Nine advisories were published on 29 September for libraries HydraOps ships, three of them high-severity in Electron (windows opened from sandboxed pages, cross-origin reads through the file/HTTP handlers, Node.js in `<webview>` workers). This release moves to `electron` 41.10.7, `brace-expansion` 5.0.12, `fast-uri` 4.2.1 and `js-yaml` 5.4.2. Nothing else changes; updating is recommended.
