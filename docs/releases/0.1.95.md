- 📬 **Gmail: new mail handed to an agent.** Each account's row in Tools → Gmail gets **Hand over new mail**: every new message that matches your filter becomes a task for the agent you choose, the way a Telegram message does, and Telegram tells you who wrote and about what. The filter is a Gmail search of your own (`in:inbox is:unread` to begin with; narrow it with a label, a sender, `-category:promotions`). You choose what the agent does: only tell you who wrote and what they want, or also leave the answer as a draft in Gmail when the message deserves one. It never sends: that still waits for your approval of the exact message. What the email says reaches the agent marked as outside content, so a message that gives the agent orders is reported to you, not obeyed.
- 🧮 **A daily limit and no replay.** Every message that becomes a task costs what the agent's model costs, so each account has a limit per day (50 by default): what arrives after it is counted in the row and left alone. Checking is free, every 10 minutes by default, and a check with nothing new is a single empty search. Switching it on, or changing the filter, starts from that moment; mail that arrives while HydraOps is closed is handed over at the next check.
- 🧵 **Bursts keep their order in the chat.** Tasks created in the same second (several new emails, several mentions on X) could show in reverse order; they now appear as they arrived.
