- ↔️ **The sidebar says it collapses.** It always did, by clicking the logo; now the logo row shows a chevron and a tooltip, and the chevron points outward when the bar is collapsed.
- 👥 **The agents rail, collapsed, keeps the agents.** Instead of 48 empty pixels you see each agent's avatar with its status dot and unread count; a click opens that agent's chat and the tooltip gives the name and status. The agent whose chat is open is marked in the rail, collapsed or not, with a darker background and an accent edge.
- 🗂️ **The chat's tab bar.** Its scrollbar sat over the underline of the active tab; the bar now hides it, scrolls sideways with the mouse wheel (and by touch), and brings the active tab into view whenever it changes, so with many agents the open chat is never past the edge.
