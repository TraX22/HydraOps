- 🔗 **Links can no longer take over the app window.** Clicking a link in an agent's reply used to navigate the HydraOps window itself to that site, with no way back to your chats. Links now always open in your browser (or a new tab, when you use HydraOps from a browser) and the app window stays on the app.
- 🛟 **No more blank window.** If the interface cannot load because the services are still starting, the window shows a "Starting HydraOps…" screen and reloads by itself as soon as they are ready, instead of staying blank.
- 🧩 **Safer start-up next to other copies.** The desktop app only reuses services that really are its own: a service is adopted by its port, and processes from another checkout or install are ignored. This fixes the app starting without its API when a developer stack was running on the same machine.
