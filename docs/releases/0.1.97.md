- 📘 **Gmail: the Google Cloud steps, as the console asks for them today.** The guide said "publish the app to Production" as if it were one button. It is not: Google keeps **Publish app** disabled until **Branding** has a home page, a privacy policy link and the domain of both. Step 1 of the Gmail guide (English and Spanish) was rewritten after doing the whole setup in a real project: the project, the Gmail API, *Google Auth Platform → Get started*, Branding, *Audience → Publish app*, and the client under *Clients → Create client → Desktop app*.
- 🔑 **Two things worth knowing before you start.** Google shows the client secret once (download the JSON, or add a new secret later), and a new client can take from five minutes to a few hours to work: if connecting fails right away, try again later.
- 💬 **The Gmail card's help text** names the same path, in the five languages.
