- ✉️ **Direct messages on X.** An account connected for *Messages* (your app at X's "Read and write and Direct message" level) reads its inbox and conversations with `x_dm` and sends a message to a user or into a conversation. Sending waits for you every time, like a post: the card and the Telegram notice show the text and who it goes to, and only the exact message you approved goes out. An incoming message never gets an answer on its own: switch "Notify direct messages" on in the account's row and each new one becomes a notice in the chat and on Telegram, the agent told to do nothing until you ask; when you ask for a reply, it is sent under your approval. Each check costs one message read even when nothing is new (X always returns the newest), so the default interval is an hour, and the row says so.
- 📋 **Lists, Spaces, communities and trends.** `x_lists` reads the account's lists, any list, its posts and members, and creates, changes, deletes, follows, pins a list or adds and removes members under your approval (with "approve for the rest of the task"). `x_explore` reads a Space, a user's Spaces, a Spaces search, a community, a communities search and what is trending in the world, a country or a city, or the account's personalized trends. Reading lists and Spaces needs two permissions X added recently: an account connected before shows a line in its row to connect it again and keeps everything else meanwhile.
- 💬 **Sign in with ChatGPT works with any plan OpenAI enables for it.** The card, the docs and the module said Plus or Pro; OpenAI now includes Codex sign-in across plans, Free and Go among them, and a Go plan works. The texts say so; usage limits depend on the plan.
