- 📅 **The Calendar view.** Complementos → Calendar shows the shared calendar: a mini month with a dot per owner and the agenda of the coming days on the left; month, week and day in the middle, with search and a New entry button; the selected entry or the form on the right (note or action, title, date, time or all day, repeat until a date, time zone, the agent and its task, notes). Owner colors; a square is an action, a circle a note; done, missed and removed entries show greyed; double-click a day to create there. The detail shows when and where, the owner, the agent and approval of an action, the last run and its task, the reserved attachments; Edit and Remove.
- 🕒 **The calendar's time zone** is set in Settings (the system's unless you pick one); each entry keeps the zone it was created in.
- ✅ **A readable approval card** when an agent schedules, changes or removes a calendar entry: what, when, which agent, the task text, the attachments, and what approval does — not a list of arguments.
- 📨 **A task the calendar started** shows in the agent's chat as your message with a "From the calendar" mark and the prompt as written; the agent is told it was scheduled. Telegram notices from the calendar come in the machine's language (`HYDRA_LANG` overrides).
- 🧩 **Skills: Update and Delete answer in the row.** The outcome ("up to date · b7cc0c0", "updated…", or the error) shows under that skill's own buttons; before, it went to a notice far above the list and a click looked like nothing happened. A skill may be up to 1 MB (was 512 KB): `unity-cli` from Unity's official skills now installs.
