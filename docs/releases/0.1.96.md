- 🗂️ **Gmail: the agents keep your mailbox in order.** A new permission per account, **Organize**, and two tools split by what they do to *your* view of the mail. `gmail_organize` adds marks and brings mail back: one of your labels on or off a message, a star, the important mark, unread again, back to the inbox, out of the bin, a new label. Nothing it does hides a message, so it asks for no approval: "label everything from Acme as Clients" just happens. `gmail_tidy` takes mail out of view: archive, mark as read, move to the bin. Every call waits for you, and the card (and the Telegram notice) lists each message it names with its sender, subject and date.
- ✅ **A clean-up asks once; the bin asks every time.** Archiving and marking as read can be approved for the rest of the task. Moving to the bin is approved call by call, and nothing deletes for good: Gmail keeps the bin for 30 days and the agent can bring a message back. An email that tells the agent to put your bank's warnings away gets nowhere: what hides mail is yours to decide, with the messages in front of you.
- ℹ️ **About Google's permission.** To put a label on a message Google offers one scope only, and its consent screen words it as "read, compose and send". HydraOps asks for it only when you tick Organize and uses it only for organizing: an account with Organize alone can neither draft nor send. To add it to an account already connected, connect it again with Organize ticked (its row offers that).
