- 🔐 **Your ChatGPT plan as a model source.** With ChatGPT Plus or Pro you can now connect HydraOps to your subscription instead of an OpenAI API key, through OpenAI's "Sign in with ChatGPT" for open-source apps. Config gets a section to connect from the browser, see the account and the models the plan serves, and disconnect; the models appear in the selector as "ChatGPT (plan)", each agent picks one, the chat says a reply came from the plan at no per-token cost, and the weekly cap you set in ChatGPT is reported in the chat when reached. Tokens stay on your computer, handled by the key-proxy.
- 🧊 **3D models, images and videos get a card in the chat.** A model a task delivers (GLB) shows a card with its size and triangle count and a viewer that opens on request; an image or a video a task brings gets a card with a preview and a download.
- 🎨 **"To Blender" from the card.** A 3D model can be sent straight into your open Blender through a Blender connection (BlenderLab), without going through a model.
- ⏻ **/exit and /restart.** Shut down or restart HydraOps from the chat, Telegram or a CLI. Both describe what would happen (tasks in progress, scheduled tasks, how to come back) and wait 60 seconds for a confirmation that only the person who asked can give; the app shows a card with the buttons.
